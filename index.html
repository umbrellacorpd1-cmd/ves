How to start

In the city in four steps

  1. 01

    Get GTA V

    You need a licensed copy of Grand Theft Auto V on PC — Steam, Epic Games or Rockstar Launcher.

  2. 02

    Install the client

    Download the multiplayer client and point it at your GTA V folder.

    Download client →
  3. 03

    Connect to AceG

    Find AceG in the server list or connect directly with the address below.

  4. 04

    Create your character

    Sign up in game, design your character and step off the plane into the city.

FAQ

Good to know

Is it free to play?

Yes. You only need your own licensed copy of GTA V on PC.

I've never played roleplay. Can I join?

Yes. Read the server rules first, then ask in Discord if anything is unclear — new players are welcome.

Where do I find the rules?

The rules are published in our Discord. Reading them before your first session saves you from an early ban.

I can't connect. What should I do?

Check that GTA V is up to date and the client points at the right game folder, then open a ticket in Discord.
